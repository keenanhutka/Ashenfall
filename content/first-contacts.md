# First contacts

No shared language. Gesture, gift, steel, or flight.

## Coast — Goblin scavengers

Cove of driftwood and hide-scraps. Five or six small figures.
Choices: hold and watch (+1), offer goods (+1), try to speak (0), seize/fight and win (+2, possible captive), leave (0).
Companions: Eadric tense, Godric names them thieves, Wulfric steps too soon, Osric watches hands and rocks.

## Trails — Orc hunting party

Four or five hunters with a carcass. They measure you.
Choices: stand (+1), offer a share (+1), speak (0), fight and win (+2), withdraw (0).
Alone, holding the trail is how you get hurt.

## Old timber — Elf watchers

Seen late. They point you back.
Choices: turn back (+1), leave a gift (+1), speak (0), push and win (+2 and a grudge), watch unseen (+1), caught hiding (0).
Capture here poisons the route.

## Rocky fold — Dwarf workings

A cut in the stone. A workplace you walked into.
Choices: stay out (+1), offer and step back (+1), mime (0), force and win (+2, tools and a grudge), leave (0).

## Stream-mouth — Troll

One creature feeding. Not a people.
Choices: slow retreat (+1), throw food and run (+1 or 0), shout (0 or +1), fight and drive/kill (+2), watch from cover (+1).
Never a captive.
