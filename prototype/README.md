# Ashenfall v0.01 prototype

Thin browser prototype of the opening loop.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (double-click, or any static server).

On start you name yourself and the house.

## What is implemented

- Day cycle, 6 AP (4 if tired)
- Assign Eadgyth, Eadric, Godric, Wulfric, Osric
- Fish, wood, raise a store-shed (assign two people to `shed` and end the day, or help yourself)
- Five explore routes and first contacts
- Capture if you win with a companion and have rope
- Convert shed to hut
- Collar from hide
- Night watch and escape
- Dock-thief if coast night-sign is ignored
- Oswin after two expeditions (tell him or keep silent; he may buy a captive)
- Starve in three empty days and you lose

## What is not in this thin build

- Palisade / Expansion 1 map swap
- Cuthwin, Hilda, Aldred arriving as playable bodies
- Saewyn romance
- Full repeat tables on every route
- Player stat creation

Raise the shed first. Assign Osric and Eadric to `shed` for a day, or spend AP on timber plus one assigned worker.
