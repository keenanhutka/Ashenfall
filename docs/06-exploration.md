# Exploration

Cost 3 AP. Player + 1 or 2 companions.

Eadgyth, first time: do not go alone. Capture needs extra hands.

## Routes

1. Follow the coast — first contact goblins
2. Take the game trails — first contact orcs
3. Push into the old timber — first contact elves
4. Climb the rocky fold — first contact dwarves
5. Follow the stream to the sea — first contact troll

Full scenes: `content/first-contacts.md`.
Repeat tables: `content/repeat-tables.md`.

Home by dusk if clean. Hard fight or long flight: Tired tomorrow.
