# Resources and production

## Piles

Food, Wood, Hide, Iron, Silver.

Food: 1 per person per day.
Wood: buildings and fire.
Hide: collars, cuffs.
Iron: tools, nails, bars.
Silver: Oswin and Saewyn.

## Day 1 kit

- Food 48 (8 days for 6)
- Wood 4
- Hide 2
- Iron 1
- Silver 12
- Rope 2

Axes and dock lines exist. No spare armoury.
