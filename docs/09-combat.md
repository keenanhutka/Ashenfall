# Combat (v0.01)

Not a war sim. When a scene fights: Stand / Drive off / Run.

Compare side weight: You Heavy / Even / You Light.

- Heavy (two sound companions vs goblins): win. Rare light wound if sloppy.
- Even (orc party, or goblins and you alone): win, lose, or break. Winner takes a wound. Lose means you run and someone is hurt.
- Light (troll, closed orc ranks): run is sense. Stand risks a companion's life.

## Injury

Hurt: 3 days poor work, no good watch. Explore anyway and combat is one step worse.
Badly hurt: 7 days, no explore, no watch.
Dead: gone. Mood drop. Eadric's death hits the player specially. Eadgyth's death cracks the hall.
Wulfric and Godric die easier than Osric.
