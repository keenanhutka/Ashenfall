# Characters

## Creation

Prompt at start: player given name + house name.

Default heir (age 18):
Strength 6, Agility 6, Intelligence 6, Charisma 7, Endurance 6, Beauty 5, Body Type 5.

Optional: move up to 2 points among the five core stats. No core stat above 8 or below 4 at start. Beauty 1–7. Body Type for a man 3–10.

## Scales

- Core stats: 1–10
- Beauty: 1 Ugly … 7 Striking
- Body Type: 1 Very petite … 10 Giant
  - Women 1–6, men 3–10

## Status

Free / Indentured / Slave.
Starting six are all Free.
Taken folk enter as Slave. No shared tongue, so no indenture yet.

## Roster cap

After the estate is yours: 6–8.
Each later map expansion: +8.
Final target ~40.

Hall alone holds 6 well, 8 at a crush.
Expansion 1 yard holds about 12–14 well.

## Renown

Starts at 0. Awards +1 or +2 for notable deeds.
Free settlers do not come from Renown alone. Word must reach the sea-road through Captain Oswin (or his crew).
First free arrivals target Renown ~8–10 after that word is out.
