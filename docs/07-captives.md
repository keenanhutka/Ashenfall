# Captives

Labor is scarce until Oswin carries word. New hands before settlers are usually taken.

## Taking someone

Only after a fight is already won, or they are down.
Need a companion present, rope or leather, and space.
Trolls cannot be taken.
Goblins are the realistic first captive. Orcs are dangerous to hold.

## Space

Hall: ugly, high Escape Risk.
Converted store-shed: 1 decently, 2 at a crush.
Proper thrall-hut: 2–3, lower risk.

## Work

No language. Carry, wood, water, dirty hall work. Fish only with a guard.
Not watch. Not explore alone. Not trusted craft.

## Restraints

Start with rope only.
Action: fashion leather collar and/or cuffs. Hide 1+ , 1 work-day.
Osric or the player. Wulfric should not fit them alone.
Collar or cuffs: Escape Risk −1 step.
Both: another small step.
Never replaces a watch.

## Thrall-hut conversion

When a captive is first taken or proposed, and a store-shed exists:
Eadgyth or Osric: put them in the shed, raise a new store after.

Choices: convert shed / keep stores and hold them in the hall / refuse to keep them / wait until morning.

## Watch

Set every night.
Osric best, then player, then Eadric, Godric fair, Wulfric poor, Eadgyth last resort, nobody is reckless.
Unwatched hut mostly ignores leather and rolls high.

## Night check

Risk bands: Low / Mid / High / Near-certain.
Results: caught at the bar / caught in the yard / gone / gone and stole / violence.
Gone: off the roster. Hunt runaway is a 3 AP explore, not certain.
Escaped goblin can come back as dock-thieves. Escaped orc can bring hunters.
