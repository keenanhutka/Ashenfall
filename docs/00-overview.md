# Ashenfall v0.01 — Overview

## Pitch

Æleric exile-heir founds a coastal hall on a strange shore. Estate management by the day. A limited roster of named people assigned to work. Small-scale fights. Renown from deeds. Labor is scarce until word of the new land spreads, so taking captives is an early temptation.

## Tone

Grim. Hall-culture with a seafaring exile edge. Speech is plain. The other races are native to this land only. No shared language at first contact.

## MVP pillars

1. Day-based play with Action Points
2. Named household assigned to jobs
3. One settlement that grows in slow waves
4. Basic stores (food, wood, hide, iron, silver)
5. Small combat, not a war sim
6. Branching opening that leads from tenant-of-nothing to a walled yard

## Opening sequence

1. Land. Hall and dock only.
2. Quest: raise a store-shed.
3. Eadgyth warns you not to range alone.
4. Explore beyond the estate. First contacts.
5. Optional captive. Shed may become a thrall-hut.
6. Night watch and escape checks.
7. Captain Oswin arrives with stores. Word will leave this coast.
8. Raise a palisade when the house outgrows one roof.
9. Oswin returns with hangers-on.

## What v0.01 is not

- Other races are not playable
- No Expansion 2–3 in play
- No full market (Oswin is the market)
- No large battles
- No deep romance beyond Saewyn's hook
