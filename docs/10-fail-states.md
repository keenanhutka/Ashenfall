# Fail states

Game over:
- Player dies
- Food at 0 for 3 days with no relief
- Troll or ignored raid hits the hall and nobody is left standing
- All five starting companions dead and Renown still under 8

Not game over:
- One companion dies
- Captive escapes
- Oswin leaves cold (settlers delayed)
- A settler is turned away

## Dock raid

If coast Night-sign was ignored, or a goblin escaped:
Night event at the dock. No explore needed.
Watch on dock or gate: catch it, possible captive, +1 Renown.
No watch: Food or Hide stolen, possible wound on whoever came out late.
