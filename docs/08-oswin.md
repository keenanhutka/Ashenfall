# Captain Oswin

Old Æleric trader, loyal to the deposed house, ambitious.

## First landing

After 2–3 explorations.
Gift of modest stores. He will not winter.
If you tell him of the new races, he carries the story.
If you do not, his crew later sees something anyway (dock-thief, tree-line watchers, troll-sign, dwarf-cut). The settler clock still starts, later and uglier.

If a barred hut exists he is not shocked by thralls. He offers to buy the exotic one for the home market.

Selling gives goods or coin, not Renown.

## Second landing

After word is out and Renown is in range.
Passengers: Cuthwin and/or Hilda (labor), Aldred (adventurer), optional Saewyn (buyer).
He still sells stores and tools. He still buys stock.

## Saewyn

Age 38. Buyer first. Optional flirt if the player talks with her while she lodges.
Second private talk before he sails can open a return-hook. She will not join the household on this visit.
Eadgyth notices. Eadric hates it.
