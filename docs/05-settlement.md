# Settlement

## Start map

Coastal hall + wooden dock. Wild timber and rock. Locked art: single longhouse, dock, rocky shore.

## Quest: first store-shed

Opens Day 2–3. Full inland exploration waits on this.
Cost: Wood 2, Labor 2 days.
Rush without Osric: shed leaks, stores spoil faster.
Ignore it: food in the hall, no thrall-hut option later.

## Quest: second store-shed

Opens the morning after the first shed becomes a thrall-hut.
Same cost as the first.

## Expansion 1 trigger

All of:
- First store built (second if the first is a hut)
- Oswin has visited and left at least once
- Renown 8+
- Food buffer and wood for a wall

And at least one of: crowding, a settler turned away, or raid-sign at the dock.

## Palisade

Wood 8, Labor 8 days. Keep fishing or you starve mid-wall.
On complete: swap to Expansion 1 map.
Capacity ~12–14. Escape Risk −1 step. Roster toward first +8 band.

## Expansion 1 yard

Keeps hall and dock. Adds:
- 2 standing house-lots
- 2 empty lots
- inland gate
- existing sheds / hut

Watch-post is a **wall upgrade** (Wood 2, Labor 2), not a lot.

Empty-lot buildings:
- Second store — 2 / 2
- Smokehouse — 3 / 3
- Proper thrall-hut — 3 / 3 (holds 2–3)
- Bunk-house — 4 / 4 (sleeps 4 free)
- Workshop — 4 / 5
- Hearth-house — 3 / 3

First pair voices:
- House first: hearth-house + store
- Hands first: bunk-house + workshop
- Hold first: proper thrall-hut + store
