# Day and Action Points

## Morning (no AP)

1. Check stores and the hut.
2. Assign each companion a day job.
3. Set night watch if you will be home. Can change at dusk.

Then the player spends AP until dusk or the pool is empty. Unspent AP dies at night.

## Player AP

- Fresh: **6**
- Tired (night watch, hard fight, hard march): **4**
- Wounded or very hungry: **3**

## Costs

| Action | AP |
|--------|----|
| Help fish, gather wood, work a shed, make restraints, inspect hall/hut | 1 |
| Explore beyond the estate | 3 |
| Hunt a runaway | 3 |
| Hard fight on a trip | next day Tired |
| Rest early | 0 |

## Companions

Companions have jobs, not AP.
Take **1 or 2** on an explore. Those people do not also finish a shed-day.
Night watch is an assignment. That person is tired tomorrow. If the player watched, player has 4 AP tomorrow.
