# Setting

## The settlers

In-game culture name: **Æleric**.
Do not call them English in game text.

Flavor: Old English / Anglo-Saxon names and household tone. Viking element kept in the exile, the crossing, and the hard landing.

The player is heir of a deposed king, banished with family and companions. All who land with him are Free.

## The new land

A wild coastal frontier (alt-history North America analogue). Rocky shore, dense timber, game trails, stone folds, a stream-mouth near the hall.

## Races

Full list for later builds: Human, Orc, Goblin, Elf, Dwarf, Troll.

v0.01 playable: **Human / Æleric only**.

All non-human races are unique to the new land. No prior familiarity. No shared language.

First impressions:
- Goblins take what is left loose
- Orcs already hunt this ground
- Elves mark the deep woods
- Dwarves work the stone
- Trolls are disasters, not tribes
